About Me

Guiding Meaningful, Lasting Transformation

“Meaningful recovery begins with awareness of one’s inner life and the courage to confront destructive beliefs and behaviors. Through education, community, and compassionate guidance, individuals can rebuild integrity, rediscover purpose, and create lasting change.”

For more than two decades, I have dedicated my work to helping individuals overcome addiction, destructive thinking patterns, and self-limiting behaviors. My focus is on helping people understand the vital relationship between their thought life, their behavior, and their ability to build lasting recovery.

I am the founder of Thought-Life Connection, a recovery and personal transformation program that integrates cognitive awareness, spiritual development, and behavioral change. Through workshops, group discussions, retreats, and one-on-one mentorship, I help individuals recognize destructive thinking patterns and develop healthier ways of living.

My approach combines practical recovery principles, including insights from the 12-Step tradition, with psychological awareness and personal responsibility. The goal is to help individuals build sustainable change by transforming the way they think about themselves, their circumstances, and their relationships.

Over the years, I have worked with people from diverse backgrounds facing addiction, trauma, and behavioral health challenges. Through compassionate guidance and structured learning, anyone can rediscover purpose and build a new path forward.
